Climate Awareness Kenya

Act today for a greener tomorrow

Quick Facts

  • Kenya aims to plant 15 billion trees by 2032.
  • About 90% of Kenya's electricity comes from renewable sources.
  • Droughts and floods are becoming more frequent.
  • Mount Kenya's glaciers have been shrinking for decades.

Why Climate Action Matters

Climate change affects farming, water supply and livelihoods across Kenya. Changing rainfall patterns make it harder for farmers to plan planting seasons, while rising temperatures put pressure on water sources and wildlife.

Every person can help. Planting trees, saving energy, reducing waste and supporting clean energy are small steps that add up to real change in our communities.

What You Can Do

Join a local tree-planting drive, switch to energy-saving bulbs, use public transport where possible, and share what you learn with friends and family.
